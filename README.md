# Student Mental Health Support and Stress Assessment Chatbot

## 📌 Project Description

The Student Mental Health Support and Stress Assessment Chatbot is a non-diagnostic application designed to help students understand and reflect on their stress levels.

Many students hesitate to seek help for stress and mental health concerns because of stigma, fear, or lack of awareness. This project provides a simple chatbot-based platform where students can interact anonymously and receive helpful resources and support information.

> **Note:** This chatbot does not provide medical diagnosis or professional medical advice.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Help students assess and understand their stress levels.
- Provide a simple and user-friendly chatbot interface.
- Share useful mental health resources and support information.
- Encourage students to seek appropriate support when needed.
- Collect anonymised feedback to improve the system.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The system uses a chatbot that asks users simple questions related to stress and well-being. Based on the responses, the chatbot provides general feedback and suggests appropriate resources or support options.

The platform is designed to be easy to use, accessible, and supportive for students.
