# VoicePay – Easy UPI for Everyone

## Project Overview
VoicePay is a voice-guided UPI assistance application designed to help elderly and low-literacy users understand and perform digital payment tasks with confidence. The system provides simple voice-based guidance, explains common UPI prompts, and promotes safe digital payment practices.

---

## Problem Statement
Many elderly and low-literacy users face difficulties while using digital banking and UPI services due to complex interfaces, unfamiliar terminology, and fear of making mistakes. This often leads to dependence on others and increases the risk of fraud.

---

## Objectives
- Help elderly and low-literacy users understand and perform common UPI tasks.
- Provide simple voice-guided assistance for digital payments.
- Reduce dependence on others for routine digital transactions.
- Improve awareness of digital payment safety and fraud prevention.
- Promote financial inclusion and digital independence.

---

## Proposed Solution
VoicePay provides step-by-step voice guidance for common UPI-related activities. Users can interact with the system using voice commands, receive spoken instructions, understand payment prompts, and learn safe digital payment practices.

---

## Features
- Voice-guided UPI assistance
- Speech-to-Text support
- Text-to-Speech responses
- Simple and accessible user interface
- Fraud awareness and safety tips
- Support for first-time digital payment users

## System Workflow

1. User opens VoicePay.
2. User gives a voice command.
3. Speech-to-Text converts speech into text.
4. VoicePay identifies the requested UPI task.
5. The system provides step-by-step voice guidance.
6. User completes the UPI task.
7. VoicePay shares safety tips and fraud alerts.
